# Wine Labs

Use `search_winelabs_endpoints` to find the right operation before calling `fetch_winelabs_data` or `fetch_winelabs_data_bundle`. Confirm the wine identity, vintage, currency and market when relevant. Cite source URLs and dates returned by the service and distinguish current offers from historical prices and auction results.

Account permissions, plan and credits govern access. Before any account-scoped create, update or other write operation, explain the action and obtain the user's confirmation. Do not request or paste OAuth tokens or login codes into chat. If access or a tool fails, report the actual error rather than inventing data.
